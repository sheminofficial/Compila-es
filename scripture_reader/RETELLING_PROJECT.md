# The Bible, Retold — Story-First English Reader

This is a **separate literary-adaptation project**, not a change to the Falelo or Compila-es production code.

## Goal

Recast all 66 Protestant-canon books / all 1,189 chapters as accessible English prose for a teenage English learner interested in Jesus and Christian teachings.

## Source and rights

- Source: official World English Bible, Protestant edition, public domain: https://ebible.org/engwebp/copyright.htm
- The source translation's text is not to be distributed under the name “World English Bible” if modified; that name is retained only for attribution of the source.
- A retelling is **not** a Bible translation. Always mark editorial speech, inferred emotion, scene setting, and condensed passages as adaptation.

## Delivered locally in ChatGPT conversation (2026-11-04)

- EPUB: `The_Bible_A_Story_First_Retelling_English.epub`
- Structural coverage: **66 books; 1,189 chapters**
- Condensed and vocabulary-annotated prose for each original chapter
- **12 fully written literary chapters** (Genesis 1–3, Exodus 3, Mark 1 and 4, Luke 2 and 15, John 3 and 4, Acts 9, Revelation 21)
- **31 scene introductions** with bespoke editorial text
- **99 clickable dictionary definitions** written in easy English, sometimes with Portuguese notes
- One verified direct scene video from the official English channel of *The Chosen* (John 4): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ordhsDeAt60
- EPUB integrity validation: ZIP CRC, EPUB mimetype/container manifest, XHTML well-formedness, complete 66-book/1,189-chapter navigation.

## Editorial honesty / outstanding work

The EPUB covers the full canon **by chapter**, but most chapters were condensed and lightly rephrased computationally. **This does not constitute a finished, thoroughly edited literary rewriting of all biblical material.** Do not advertise it as human-reviewed or as equivalent to a comprehensive new translation. Precise verse-level statements, difficult ancient passages, and theological wording should be compared against the original Bible. Do not add invented dialogue and then attribute it to Scripture.

The author's actual finished literary goal is a chapter-by-chapter storytelling edition written and checked for fidelity throughout, not an annotated Bible containing unchanged WEB verses.
