# 1 John

Editorial manuscript for *The Bible, Retold - A Literary Adaptation for Young English Readers*. The public-domain Protestant World English Bible is the source. Read this adaptation alongside Scripture.

## Chapter 1 — The Word of Life

The writers proclaimed what existed from the beginning: what they had heard, seen with their eyes, watched, and touched concerning the Word of life. The life appeared; they had seen it and testified to the eternal life that was with the Father and appeared to them.

They announced what they had seen and heard so readers could share fellowship with them - fellowship with the Father and his Son, Jesus Christ - and so their joy might be complete.

The message was this: God is light, and no darkness exists in him. Claiming fellowship while walking in darkness was a lie. Walking in the light created fellowship with one another, and Jesus' blood cleansed from every sin.

Anyone claiming to have no sin deceived himself, and truth was absent. If believers confessed sins, God was faithful and just to forgive and cleanse them from all wrongdoing. Claiming never to have sinned made God a liar and rejected his word.

*Original Scripture [1 John 1](https://ebible.org/engwebp/1JN01.htm).*

## Chapter 2 — Walk as Jesus Walked

John wrote so they would not sin. If anyone sinned, Jesus Christ the righteous was their advocate with the Father and the atoning sacrifice for the whole world.

Knowing God appeared through obedience. A person claiming to know him while ignoring commands lied. Whoever said he remained in Christ should walk as Jesus walked.

The command was both old and new: love. Darkness was passing and true light shining. Hatred kept a person in darkness; loving a brother or sister meant living in light.

John addressed little children whose sins were forgiven, fathers who knew the One from the beginning, and young people who overcame the evil one through God's abiding word.

They must not love the world's rebellious desires, craving eyes, or pride in possessions. The world passes away, but whoever does God's will remains.

Many anticrists had appeared, showing it was the last hour. They departed because they never truly belonged. Believers had an anointing from the Holy One and knew truth. The liar denied Jesus as Christ and denied Father and Son.

They should let the original message remain within them. Then they would remain in the Son and Father and receive promised eternal life. Christ's anointing taught them to remain in him, so they could be confident at his appearing.

*Original Scripture [1 John 2](https://ebible.org/engwebp/1JN02.htm).*

## Chapter 3 — Children of God Love in Action

The Father's love was astonishing: believers were called God's children. The world did not recognize them because it did not know him. What they would become had not fully appeared, but when Christ appeared they would be like him, seeing him as he is. This hope led toward purity.

Sin was lawlessness. Christ appeared to remove sins, and no sin was in him. A life settled in sin contradicted knowing him. The devil had sinned from the beginning, but God's Son appeared to destroy his works.

God's children and the devil's children became evident through righteousness and love. The message from the beginning was to love one another, unlike Cain, who murdered his brother because his own works were evil and his brother's righteous.

Believers should not be surprised by the world's hatred. Love showed passage from death to life; hatred resembled murder.

Jesus revealed love by laying down his life. His people should likewise give themselves for one another. If someone with resources closed his heart against a needy brother or sister, how could God's love remain? They must love not merely in words but in action and truth.

This reassured their hearts before God. Even when conscience condemned, God was greater and knew everything. Confidence grew through believing in Jesus' name and loving one another. Obedient believers remained in God, and God in them, known through the Spirit he gave.

*Original Scripture [1 John 3](https://ebible.org/engwebp/1JN03.htm).*

## Chapter 4 — Test the Spirits

They must not believe every spirit but test them, because many false prophets had entered the world. The Spirit from God confessed that Jesus Christ came in flesh. The spirit denying Jesus was the antichrist's spirit already active in the world.

Believers belonged to God and had overcome false teachers because the One within them was greater than the one in the world. False teachers spoke from the world and the world listened; those knowing God listened to apostolic truth.

They should love one another because love comes from God. God showed love by sending his only Son so people might live through him. Love began not with their affection for God but with God's gift of his Son as an atoning sacrifice.

Since God loved them, they must love one another. Though no one had seen God, mutual love made his love complete among them. The Spirit testified that they remained in God. The witnesses had seen and declared the Father sent the Son as Savior of the world.

God is love. Whoever remained in love remained in God. Mature love gave confidence for judgment because perfect love drove out fear of punishment.

They loved because God first loved them. Claiming love for God while hating a visible brother was a lie. Whoever loves God must love brother and sister also.

*Original Scripture [1 John 4](https://ebible.org/engwebp/1JN04.htm).*

## Chapter 5 — The Testimony of Eternal Life

Everyone believing Jesus is the Christ has been born of God. Loving the Father includes loving his children. Love for God means keeping commands, which are not crushing, because faith overcomes the world.

Jesus Christ came by water and blood, not water alone, and the Spirit testified because the Spirit is truth. Spirit, water, and blood agreed.

Human testimony is accepted, but God's testimony is greater: he testified about his Son. Whoever trusts the Son carries this testimony within; rejecting it makes God a liar. The testimony is that God gave eternal life, and this life is in his Son. Whoever has the Son has life.

John wrote so believers would know they had eternal life. They could approach God confidently: requests according to his will were heard.

If someone saw a brother committing sin not leading to death, he should pray and God would give life. There was sin leading to death; John distinguished it without making every wrongdoing harmless, for all wrongdoing is sin.

Those born of God do not settle into sin; God's Son guards them, and the evil one cannot seize them. They belonged to God while the whole world lay under evil power.

God's Son had come and given understanding to know the True One. Believers were in him through Jesus Christ, the true God and eternal life.

Little children, guard yourselves from idols.

*Original Scripture [1 John 5](https://ebible.org/engwebp/1JN05.htm).*
