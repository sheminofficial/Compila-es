# Ephesians

Editorial manuscript for *The Bible, Retold - A Literary Adaptation for Young English Readers*. The public-domain Protestant World English Bible is the source. Read this adaptation alongside Scripture.

## Chapter 1 @@ Every Spiritual Blessing

Paul, an apostle by God's will, wrote grace and peace to the faithful in Christ.

God had blessed them in Christ with every spiritual blessing. Before creation he chose them to be holy and adopted them through Jesus. In the Beloved they had redemption through his blood and forgiveness according to overflowing grace.

God revealed the mystery of his purpose: at the right time everything in heaven and earth would be united in Christ. In him believers received an inheritance, and those who heard and trusted the gospel were sealed with the promised Holy Spirit, the guarantee of what God would complete.

Having heard of their faith and love, Paul continually gave thanks. He prayed that the Father of glory would give wisdom and revelation, illuminating their hearts to know the hope of his call, the riches of his inheritance, and the immeasurable power working for believers.

That power raised Christ from death and seated him above every ruler, authority, power, dominion, and name, in this age and the next. God placed all things under Christ's feet and made him head over the church, his body, the fullness of the One filling all things.

*Original Scripture [Ephesians 1](https://ebible.org/engwebp/EPH01.htm).*

## Chapter 2 @@ One New Humanity

They had been dead in wrongdoing, following the world's course, the ruler of the air, and selfish desires. Like everyone else, they were under judgment.

But God, rich in mercy and great love, made them alive with Christ, raised them, and seated them with him, displaying grace for ages to come. They were saved by grace through faith, not from themselves or works, so no one could boast. They were God's workmanship, created in Christ for good works prepared beforehand.

Gentile believers once lacked circumcision, Christ, Israel's citizenship, covenants, hope, and God. Now Christ's blood brought them near.

Christ was their peace. He broke the dividing wall and hostility, creating Jews and Gentiles into one new humanity through the cross. He came preaching peace to far and near; through him both had access to the Father by one Spirit.

They were no longer strangers but fellow citizens and members of God's household, built upon apostles and prophets with Christ as cornerstone. The whole structure grew into a holy temple, a dwelling for God by the Spirit.

*Original Scripture [Ephesians 2](https://ebible.org/engwebp/EPH02.htm).*

## Chapter 3 @@ Strength to Know Christ's Love

Paul was Christ's prisoner for the nations. God's grace commissioned him with a mystery revealed by the Spirit: Gentiles were fellow heirs, members of one body, and sharers in Christ's promise through the gospel.

Though considering himself least among God's people, Paul received grace to announce Christ's unsearchable riches and reveal the hidden plan. Through the church, God's many-sided wisdom was now displayed to rulers and authorities in heavenly places, according to his eternal purpose in Christ. In Christ believers approached God with bold confidence.

Paul asked them not to lose heart over his sufferings, which served their glory.

He bowed before the Father, from whom every family receives its name. He prayed they would be strengthened through the Spirit in their inner person, that Christ would dwell in their hearts through faith, and that, rooted in love, they might grasp its width, length, height, and depth.

They should know Christ's love, though it surpasses knowledge, and be filled with God's fullness. Glory belonged in the church and Christ to the God able to do immeasurably beyond all they asked or imagined.

*Original Scripture [Ephesians 3](https://ebible.org/engwebp/EPH03.htm).*

## Chapter 4 @@ Grow into Christ

As the Lord's prisoner, Paul urged them to live worthily, with humility, gentleness, patience, and loving tolerance, preserving the Spirit's unity in peace.

There was one body, Spirit, hope, Lord, faith, baptism, and God and Father of all. Yet Christ gave varied grace. Having descended and ascended above the heavens, he gave apostles, prophets, evangelists, shepherds, and teachers to equip God's people for service and build Christ's body.

They should mature into Christ's fullness, no longer tossed like children by deceptive teaching. Speaking truth in love, every connected part would help the body grow in love.

They must no longer live in futile, darkened thinking. Having learned Christ, they should remove the old self corrupted by deceitful desires, renew their minds, and put on the new self created in righteousness and holiness.

Therefore they should replace lies with truth, control anger before sunset, stop stealing and work generously, reject corrupt speech and speak what builds others. They must not grieve the Holy Spirit. Bitterness, rage, shouting, slander, and malice should give way to kindness, compassion, and forgiveness as God forgave them in Christ.

*Original Scripture [Ephesians 4](https://ebible.org/engwebp/EPH04.htm).*

## Chapter 5 @@ Walk in Love and Light

As beloved children they should imitate God and walk in love, as Christ loved them and gave himself as a fragrant offering.

Sexual immorality, impurity, greed, filthy talk, and crude joking did not fit God's people; thanksgiving did. No deceiver should persuade them that such rebellion carried no judgment.

Once they were darkness; now they were light in the Lord. They should live as children of light, producing goodness, righteousness, and truth, exposing fruitless darkness. Awake, sleeper, rise from the dead, and Christ will shine.

They must walk wisely, redeeming time because days were evil. Instead of drunkenness, they should be filled with the Spirit, singing psalms, hymns, and spiritual songs, thanking God and submitting to one another.

Wives were called to submit to husbands as the church to Christ. Husbands must love wives as Christ loved the church and gave himself to sanctify her. Loving a wife was loving one's own body; no one hates his flesh but nourishes it.

Marriage echoed the mystery of Christ and the church. Each husband must love his wife as himself, and the wife respect her husband.

*Original Scripture [Ephesians 5](https://ebible.org/engwebp/EPH05.htm).*

## Chapter 6 @@ Stand in God's Armor

Children should obey parents in the Lord. Honoring father and mother was the first command with a promise. Fathers must not provoke children but raise them in the Lord's instruction.

Enslaved people were told to obey earthly masters sincerely as serving Christ, not merely while watched. Masters must treat them likewise, stop threatening, and remember that their shared Master in heaven showed no favoritism.

Finally, believers should become strong in the Lord. They must put on God's full armor to stand against the devil's schemes, for their struggle was not merely human but against spiritual rulers, authorities, darkness, and evil forces.

They should stand with truth as a belt, righteousness as a breastplate, gospel peace as footwear, faith as a shield extinguishing flaming arrows, salvation as a helmet, and God's word as the Spirit's sword.

They should pray at all times in the Spirit, remain alert, and intercede for all believers and for Paul, that he might boldly announce the gospel mystery as an ambassador in chains.

Tychicus, the beloved brother and faithful servant, would explain Paul's circumstances and encourage them. Peace, love with faith, and grace belonged to all who loved Jesus Christ with undying love.

*Original Scripture [Ephesians 6](https://ebible.org/engwebp/EPH06.htm).*
